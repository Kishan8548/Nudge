# 🎙️ Nudge AI: The Ultimate Demo Recording Script (Monologue)

*This script is perfectly designed to be recorded as a solo voice memo (MP3/WAV) and uploaded via the "Upload Meeting" page. It deliberately triggers **every single feature** of the Nudge AI pipeline, including the "Needs Human Review" fallback!*

---

### 🎬 The Script (Read by you or your friend)

"Alright team, thanks for joining the Q3 Marketing Sync. Let's get right into it. 

First, I want to confirm that we have made a final decision on the advertising budget. We are officially increasing the Q3 ad spend by 15% to support the new feature launch. So that decision is locked in.

Now for some action items. Suren, I need you to finalize the new landing page designs. Please make sure those are completed and sent to me by tomorrow afternoon. 

And I'll take the action item to update the master budget spreadsheet on my end by Friday morning. 

Oh, one last thing... someone needs to call the catering company to organize food for the launch party next week. I'm not sure who's handling that, but can somebody please just get that done soon? 

I think that covers everything for today. Thanks everyone, talk to you later!"

---

### 🧠 What this script proves to the Judges:
When you upload this recording to Nudge, the AI will perfectly extract:
1. **The Decision:** "Officially increasing the Q3 ad spend by 15%."
2. **Action Item 1 (High Confidence):** Suren to finalize landing page designs by tomorrow afternoon.
3. **Action Item 2 (High Confidence):** Manager/Speaker to update the budget spreadsheet by Friday morning.
4. **The "Wow" Factor (Human-in-the-Loop):** The final task ("somebody please call the catering company soon") has **no clear owner** and **no clear deadline**. The AI will detect this, lower its confidence score, and flag the meeting with a yellow **"Needs Human Review"** badge on your dashboard, proving that the agent is intelligent enough to know when it needs human help!
5. **The Summary:** The Groq LLM will generate a perfect 3-sentence executive summary about the Q3 marketing sync and budget increase.
